Travel Weather Guide

The Four Enchanting Seasons

Kashmir transforms dramatically with each passing season. Choose your favorite hue of paradise!

March — May

Spring Blossoms

🌡️ 14°C to 22°C • Pleasant

Asia’s largest Tulip Garden blooms in Srinagar with over 1.5 million colorful tulips. Almond and cherry blossoms blanket the valleys in pink and white.

June — August

Verdant Summer

🌡️ 20°C to 30°C • Warm & Lush

Escape the scorching plains into emerald valleys, roaring crystal rivers, alpine lake treks (Great Lakes), and apple orchards burgeoning with fresh fruit.

September — November

Golden Autumn

🌡️ 10°C to 20°C • Crisp & Crisp

Majestic royal Chinar trees turn fiery shades of crimson, amber, and gold. The world-famous purple saffron flowers bloom across the fields of Pampore.

December — February

Snow Wonderland

🌡️ -4°C to 8°C • White Snow

Gulmarg transforms into Asia's powder skiing capital. Dal Lake develops icy frost, while travelers cozy up with warm Kangris and Saffron Kahwa.
